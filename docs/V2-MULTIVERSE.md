# OpenWorld v2: the multiverse (concept, 26 Oct 2026)

Concept page (co-op study, game design, art direction, concept frames):
https://claude.ai/artifact/QRDyzK9F2NKKjxGwX6TKVg

- **One map, several universes.** Every universe keeps the same coordinates; only its parameters change
  (sea level, ice, forest, mountain scale, sky, grade). Each is labelled honestly: *Record* (what was),
  *Projection* (what science projects), *What if* (one law changed on purpose).
- **Co-op first.** Crews of 2–4 formed with a Link phrase, echoes of crew mates in other universes,
  pings, tuning circles a crew crosses together, a community Atlas, class and family rooms. No free text.
- **Art direction: cinematic.** Physically based light and atmosphere, real scale, realistic proportions,
  one magical accent (the gold rift light). Replaces the storybook look of v1.
- **Real-time prototype:** [`prototype/multiverse/`](../prototype/multiverse/) renders the concept scene in
  the browser (three.js WebGPU / WebGL 2): the four universes, the rift, the explorer.

Open decisions: production models (Blender scripts vs outsourced hero assets), whether What-if universes
ship, who owns co-op puzzle state, renaming the Idea Grove to the Nexus.
